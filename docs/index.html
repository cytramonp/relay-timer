Your meetings

One schedule. Everyone's time.

Each slot ends at a milestone from the meeting start. Finish your 8-minute turn at 7:00, and the next person gets 9 minutes until 16:00. Run to 9:00, and they get 7.

  • Use people, discussion topics, or a mix. The example agenda is editable.
  • Drag rows to reorder. Focus a drag handle and press Up or Down for keyboard control.
  • Drag a milestone boundary or edit minutes. Keep proportions shares the adjustment across unspoken slots; Equal times splits the remaining planned time equally across unfinished speakers and topics, clearing their manual pins. Completed slots stay unchanged during a session.
  • Pause freezes the meeting clock. Use −1 / +1 minute or drag its marker to correct it.
  • Floating can stay above other windows in supported browsers. A shared tab or app may exclude it: check what your audience sees.
  • Use the Templates dropdown to start a meeting, save the current agenda, or open Manage templates to customize a plan. Choose its color and icon; edit, reorder, and add slots. Each use creates a fresh meeting.
  • Your library saves automatically. Google sign-in adds private sync. Share live creates a separate live screen; its host tab must stay open.
  • Wait for the host keeps counting overtime. Auto-next advances at each deadline. Extend by 1 min adds time to this slot and the overall schedule.
  • For later keeps topics and owners private. Session report preserves the plan captured at Start and exports actual times as CSV.

Templates are starting points. Scrum's Daily is a 15-minute team event; equal time per person is optional.

Your meetings, wherever you work.

Continue with Google to sync your meeting library across devices. Meetings from this browser will be added to your private account.

The timer works without signing in. We request your basic Google profile, not access to your mail, calendar or Drive.

Your agenda stays yours.

Without sign-in, meeting names, agendas, settings and timing history stay in this browser's local storage. Export a backup before clearing browser data.

With Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication handles your basic account profile. Saved meetings and custom templates are synchronized to Cloud Firestore and protected by rules that restrict access to your Google account's user ID.

Share live is optional. It publishes the meeting title, agenda names, planned durations and current timer to anyone with its unguessable viewer link. Notes, owners, reports and Google profile are excluded. A separate control link allows next, pause, undo and extend: share it only with a trusted co-host. Stop sharing revokes both links; they expire after 24 hours.

Live hosting can use a temporary anonymous Firebase identity. No Google login is needed to watch a live screen. No advertising, analytics or payment service is included.

You can export meetings, delete them from your library, or sign out. Deleting a signed-in meeting removes its content from the cloud record; a small deletion marker prevents it from returning from another device.
