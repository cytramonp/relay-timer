How it works and advice

Step by step: Online Stopwatch

  1. Set the timing using the controls beside the clock. Choose a suitable preset when available, then adjust it to your session.
  2. If you need an alarm, choose its sound and volume and press Test sound. Turn sounds off for quiet work.
  3. Press Start to begin and Pause to hold the clock. Reset returns the session to its beginning. For a stopwatch, Lap records a split while running.
  4. Drag the timeline to correct the time, then resume when ready. Fullscreen enlarges the clock without moving the account controls into the timer.
  5. Save current as template to reuse the setup. The outlined red star adds the timer to favorites; press it again to remove it.

When to use this timer

  • Record lap splits during running practice.
  • Measure the duration of a presentation rehearsal.
  • Compare repeated attempts at the same task.

How the Online Stopwatch works

The online stopwatch measures elapsed time by counting up. Press Start to begin, Pause to freeze the reading and Resume to continue the same run. Paused time is excluded. Reset clears the elapsed time and lap list for a fresh measurement.

Record a lap at each checkpoint. Split shows the time since the previous lap, while Total shows elapsed time from the start. You can tap Lap while running or press L when keyboard focus is outside a control. The display shows tenths of a second.

Export CSV downloads the lap number, split seconds and cumulative elapsed seconds for review in a spreadsheet. The stopwatch and laps are saved in this browser; after a reload, the session returns paused. For a deadline that counts down to zero, choose Simple Timer instead.

Advice for using the Online Stopwatch

  • Decide what counts as the start and finish before measuring a task.
  • Use laps for repeated attempts or checkpoints instead of resetting between every segment.
  • Compare split times when intervals are comparable; use cumulative totals to understand the whole run.
  • Export the lap list before clearing browser data or starting a run that replaces it.
  • Treat browser timing as a practical everyday tool rather than certified precision measurement.

Useful details

Does pausing affect my lap times?

Paused time is excluded from elapsed and lap times. Resuming continues the same stopwatch run.

Are settings synchronized with my meeting account?

These standalone tools keep their own settings and saved setups in this browser. They do not use Google/Firebase meeting synchronization. Export and import a setup to transfer it to another device.

Will this timer work if I close the page?

Keep the timer tab open. Browsers can suspend background tabs, especially on phones. Elapsed time is recalculated when the page resumes, but an alarm at the exact deadline cannot be guaranteed. Reloaded interval, stopwatch and chess sessions return paused; the New Year countdown follows its selected date and time zone.
