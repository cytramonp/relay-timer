How it works and advice

How the 10 Minute Timer works

This 10 minute timer is preset to 10:00 for a short activity with a clear finish. It is useful for reading a few pages, tidying a room, practicing a passage or running a short group exercise. Open the page, check the duration and press Start when you are ready. Opening the page alone does not start the countdown.

Pause freezes the time remaining, and Resume continues the same run. Add one minute if the activity needs a small extension, or Reset to return to your configured duration. The initial duration on this page is 10 minutes; if you save or edit the setup, this browser can restore that different duration on your next visit.

Choose an alarm and volume, then use Test sound to check the finish cue. You can set a warning in seconds left and a final sequence of countdown beeps; zero disables either cue. Fullscreen makes the countdown easier to read from a distance where your browser supports it. Keep the tab open for a live alarm.

Advice for using the 10 Minute Timer

  • Define the result you want at the end of ten minutes before pressing Start.
  • For pair work, leave a little time after the timer for each pair to report back.
  • If you repeat the same activity, reset the countdown instead of re-entering the duration.
  • Test the alarm on the actual device you will use, and export a setup if you want it on another device.

Useful details

How do I restart the 10 minute countdown?

Press Reset to stop the current run and return to the duration in your setup, then press Start. If you changed the setup, choose 10 min first to restore this page’s original preset.

Are settings synchronized with my meeting account?

These standalone tools keep their own settings and saved setups in this browser. They do not use Google/Firebase meeting synchronization. Export and import a setup to transfer it to another device.

Will this timer work if I close the page?

Keep the timer tab open. Browsers can suspend background tabs, especially on phones. Elapsed time is recalculated when the page resumes, but an alarm at the exact deadline cannot be guaranteed. Reloaded interval, stopwatch and chess sessions return paused; the New Year countdown follows its selected date and time zone.
