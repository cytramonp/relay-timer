Recommended next additions, pending user choice:
1. 1/2/3-minute and 1-hour presets; then 40/45/50/90-minute and 2-hour presets.
2. Online Alarm Clock, setting a wall-clock time rather than an elapsed checkpoint.
3. Kitchen Timer with parallel dishes, strengthening the existing Egg Timer entry point.
4. Countdown to a date/event (birthday, holiday, Christmas); product recommendation, not a verified volume ranking.
5. Improve Study/Focus scenarios around the existing Pomodoro rather than creating duplicate tools.

Egg Timer is already available. The reviewed sources do not establish its exact volume or global rank. No additional timer categories were added during this research.
