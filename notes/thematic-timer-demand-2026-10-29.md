## Recommended product order

1. Kitchen Timer: independent named dishes, turn/check/rest reminders and coordination toward a common serving time. User explicitly likes this idea.
2. Meditation Timer: preparation, main session, interval bells, quiet ending and optional hidden digits.
3. Tea Brewing Timer: tea type, repeated infusions, stage-specific timing and temperature notes.
4. Toothbrushing Timer: visible zones, zone handoff and a child-friendly mode. A thematic routine, not simply another duration preset.
5. Breathing Timer: editable inhale/pause/exhale phases, cycle count and synchronized expansion/contraction. No health-effect claims are needed.
6. Yoga/Stretching: named poses, hold, left/right side and transition preparation. Not included in the Google comparison; product hypothesis.
7. Speech/Presentation: green/amber/red threshold cues and an actual rehearsal record. Not included in the Google comparison; related to Meeting/Debate but with a distinct presentation view.
